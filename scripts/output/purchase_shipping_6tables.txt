================================================================================
表关系（申购 → 采购 → 发货）
================================================================================

purchase_plan (申购单)
  └── purchase_plan_item (申购明细)  [po_plan_no 关联]
        │
        └── purchase_order_item (采购明细)  [po_plan_no + plan_row_id 关联]
              │
              └── purchase_order (采购单)  [po_no 关联]
                    │
                    └── first_leg_shipping_order_item (发货明细)  [po_no 关联]
                          │
                          └── first_leg_shipping_order (发货单)  [shipping_order_code 关联]

同时：
purchase_plan_item.source_order_code → first_leg_shipping_order_item.source_order_code (申购单号直接关联)
first_leg_shipping_order.plan_code → first_leg_shipping_order_item.order_plan_id 关联
